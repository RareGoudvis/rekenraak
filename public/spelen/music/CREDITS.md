# Muziek in de spelletjes

Background loops for the RekenRaak games (src/oefenen/spelen/music.ts). Every track is dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 by its author (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); credit is
not required, we give it gladly. Converted for the web: mono, 80 kbps mp3, loudness evened out to about
-20 LUFS, longer pieces cut to 75 s with a fade at the end (the player overlaps the passes).
